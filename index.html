ORBITAL CDN

Two thirds of the planet is far from a cloud region.

Content served from orbit can beat long-haul fibre for those users — but only if the router respects constraints that don't exist on the ground: data centres go dark in eclipse, radiation corrupts packets over the South Atlantic Anomaly, and rain closes ground gateways.

This simulator routes a real request across 180 SGP4-propagated satellites and 4 orbital data centres, then learns from every request it has served to route the next one better.
